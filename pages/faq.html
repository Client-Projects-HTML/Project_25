Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to what homeowners ask us most

How long does painting take?

A standard room takes 1-2 days; full home makeovers usually take 5-10 days depending on size and scope.

Which paint brands do you use?

We work with leading low-VOC, quality-tested brands and can match any brand you already prefer.

Can I customize wallpaper?

Yes — colors, patterns and finish can all be customized during your consultation.

Is consultation free?

Yes, our initial home visit and color consultation are completely free of charge.

Do you provide furniture setup?

Yes, our furniture decor team can style, arrange and set up furniture as part of any package.

Is there a warranty on the work?

All projects include a workmanship warranty ranging from 1 to 5 years depending on the package chosen.
