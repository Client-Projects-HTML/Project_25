About Bloom Interiors

Our story, our people, our process

Our Story

Bloom Interiors started in 2012 as a two-person painting crew with a simple idea: homes deserve the same care as any other work of art. Fourteen years later, we're a full design-and-decor studio serving families across central India.

Every project still gets the same personal attention as our very first one.

Our Mission

To make thoughtful, well-crafted interior design accessible to every homeowner — not just show homes.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted name in home painting and decor across India, one room at a time.
