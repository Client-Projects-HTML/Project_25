Our Story

Bloom Interiors started in 2012 as a two-person painting crew with a simple idea: homes deserve the same care as any other work of art. Fourteen years later, we're a full design-and-decor studio serving families across central India.

Every project still gets the same personal attention as our very first one — from the first consultation to the final walkthrough.

Our Mission

To make thoughtful, well-crafted interior design accessible to every homeowner — not just show homes. We believe beautiful spaces should be within reach for everyone.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted name in home painting and decor across India — one thoughtfully transformed room at a time.
