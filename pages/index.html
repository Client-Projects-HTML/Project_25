Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the consultation really free?

Yes — our initial home consultation and colour suggestions are completely free of charge with no obligation.

How long does a typical painting project take?

A standard room takes 1–2 days. Full home makeovers usually take 5–10 days depending on size and scope.

Do you use low-VOC or eco-friendly paints?

Yes. We are a certified Low-VOC partner and use eco-conscious paint products that are safe for families and pets.

What warranty do you offer?

Interior painting comes with a 2-year warranty; exterior painting is covered for 5 years. All work is inspected before we call it done.
